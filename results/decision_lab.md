## 1. Decision consistency

Each variant changes exactly one feature in one lane. A change is counted when the agent spares a different group than in the base dilemma. Pairs where either decision is an exact tie are excluded, because the tie-break, not the rule, decides them. A violation is a change caused by a feature the rule does not read.

## 3. Comparison with Moral Machine preference directions

Source: Awad et al. (2018), The Moral Machine experiment, Nature 563:59-64, abstract and Fig. 2a (direction only). Each contrast is run with the favoured group in the stay lane and again in the swerve lane.

| Feature | Humans tend to spare | over | strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| species | humans | pets | strong |
| count | more characters | fewer characters | strong |
| age | the young | the elderly | strong |
| lawful | people crossing legally | people crossing on red | weaker |
| role | pedestrians | passengers | weaker |
| intervention | the group saved by not swerving | the group saved by swerving | weaker |
